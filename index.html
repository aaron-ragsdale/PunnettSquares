Think like a geneticist

Teacher notes & vocabulary

Genotype is the allele combination; phenotype is the observable trait. Homozygous means two identical alleles; heterozygous means two different alleles. The simulated traits are fictional. Complete dominance means the heterozygote expresses the dominant phenotype; incomplete dominance produces an intermediate phenotype; codominance expresses both allele-associated traits distinctly. You can change the inheritance rules in this model; real organisms have biological inheritance rules that cannot be changed by a setting. Random samples may differ from theoretical probabilities.

Ask students to predict before breeding, compare samples of 10 and 100, and explain why two parents can produce different offspring. No accounts or network requests are needed; progress lasts for this page session.
